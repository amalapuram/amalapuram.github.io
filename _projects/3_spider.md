Labeling network traffic is expensive, so real deployments rarely have fully labeled data for new attack types. {% cite suresh %}, presented at **IEEE INFOCOM 2024**, introduces SPIDER, a semi-supervised continual learning-based network intrusion detection system that learns from a mix of labeled and unlabeled traffic as new threats appear.
